## Vision

### 10x Check
The current tool maps 6 hand-entered projects. The 10x version answers "has anyone here built X?" in plain language across every repo in the institution, at the moment a PI writes a grant. It also hands repo owners one-click fixes that raise their FAIR evidence. The harvest, taxonomy and relations engine already exist in the plan, so the extra cost is about 2x.

### Platonic Ideal
A PI types "segment Dutch court decisions" into the search box and sees the matching modules with their FAIR evidence and the teams behind them. One click gives them a reuse statement to paste into a grant's software management plan. The repo owner gets a PR that adds the missing CITATION.cff. A graph shows clusters across faculties, and other Dutch universities' registries can harvest the same data through codemeta feeds.
